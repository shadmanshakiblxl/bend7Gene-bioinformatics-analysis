01 / Biological Context

The BEN Domain & Functional Repression

A nuclear transcriptional repressor located on Chromosome 12 (12q23.1) in Homo sapiens and Chromosome 10 in Anas georgica, modulating chromatin accessibility in neurodevelopment.

Gene Architecture & Mechanism

BEND7 encodes a nuclear protein belonging to the BEN-domain superfamily—a class of chromatin-associated transcriptional regulators characterized by an approximately 90–100 amino-acid BEN domain (named after BANP, E5R, and NAC1). Expressed predominantly in the central nervous system (CNS) and developing embryonic tissues, BEND7 interacts with chromatin remodelling machinery (such as NuRD and SIN3A complexes) to repress gene transcription.

~100 aa BEN Domain Size

03 / Theoretical Framework

Algorithm Assumptions & Comparison

Three distinct phylogenetic algorithms were applied to evaluate topological robustness, substitution rate variation, and molecular clock sensitivity.

01 Distance-Based

Neighbor-Joining (NJ)

Saitou & Nei (1987)

Iteratively joins pairs of operational taxonomic units (OTUs) that minimize total branch length under the Q-criterion. Uses JTT substitution matrix correction.

02 Agglomerative

UPGMA

Sokal & Michener (1958)

Simple hierarchical clustering based on pairwise arithmetic means. Assumes a strict molecular clock (ultrametric tree), forcing uniform evolutionary rates.

03 Character-Based

Maximum Parsimony

Fitch (1971)

Evaluates aligned character columns directly to select the tree requiring the fewest total evolutionary amino-acid changes (parsimony steps).

Task 3: Recombinant Plasmid Construction & Host Amplification

Host Strain: E. coli DH5α (endA1 recA1)
Step Molecular Action Experimental Details & Technical Rationale
1 Template cDNA Preparation Extract total RNA from Anas georgica tissue; synthesize first-strand cDNA using SuperScript IV RT with oligo(dT) primers to represent the full mRNA transcript population.
2 High-Fidelity PCR Amplification Amplify 1,647 bp BEND7 CDS using Q5 High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase and engineered BamHI/XhoI primers. Cycling: 98°C 30s; 35 cycles of [98°C 10s, 62°C 20s, 72°C 90s]; 72°C 5m. Expected product ~1,700 bp.
3 PCR Purification Purify amplicon using spin-column cleanup (Qiagen MinElute) to eliminate excess primers, dNTPs, and polymerase. Verify yield and purity via NanoDrop (A260/280 ~1.8).
4 Double Restriction Digest (Insert) Digest purified PCR amplicon with BamHI-HF + XhoI (NEB CutSmart Buffer, 37°C, 2 hrs). Generates sticky 5'-GATC and 5'-TCGA overhangs. Heat-inactivate at 80°C for 20 mins.
5 Double Restriction Digest (Vector) Digest pET-28a-H2A.1 plasmid (Addgene #42634) with BamHI-HF + XhoI under identical conditions to release the ~369 bp H2A.1 insert and linearize the ~5,350 bp vector backbone.
6 Dephosphorylation (Optional) Treat linearized vector backbone with Calf Intestinal Alkaline Phosphatase (CIP) to prevent self-ligation of non-fully digested vector. Heat inactivate prior to ligation.
7 Gel Purification of Backbone Resolve vector digestion products on 1% agarose gel. Excise and purify the large ~5,350 bp vector backbone band, separating it completely from the small H2A.1 fragment.
8 Gel Purification of Insert Gel-purify digested BEND7 amplicon (~1,647 bp) to isolate clean cohesive-ended insert free of undigested or non-specifically amplified DNA fragments.
9 T4 DNA Ligation Ligate purified vector backbone and BEND7 insert at a 1:3 molar ratio using T4 DNA Ligase at 16°C overnight. Include vector-only and insert-only controls.
10 Transformation & Selection Transform chemically competent E. coli DH5α by heat-shock (42°C, 30s). Recover in SOC medium for 1 hr at 37°C and plate on LB agar containing 50 µg/mL Kanamycin.

06 / Structural Biology

Protein Structure & Ligand Interaction Analysis

Homology-based structural analysis of the BEND7 BEN domain fold and its physiological DNA ligand interaction using the 2.13 Å crystal structure of Human BEND6 (PDB 7YUN) as a validated paralog model.

06.1 Methodology

Homology Model Selection Rationale

PDB Entry: 7YUN (2.13 Å)

A search of the RCSB PDB for BEND7 or "BEN domain-containing protein 7" confirms that no experimentally solved structure currently exists for any organism, including Anas georgica.

Sequence similarity searches (BLASTp against PDB) identify Human BEND6 as the closest solved structural homolog. Both BEND6 and BEND7 are paralogous single-BEN-domain proteins with near-identical five-helix bundle folds. The selected reference structure, PDB 7YUN (Liu et al., 2023, J. Biol. Chem.), represents the crystal structure of the BEND6 BEN domain in complex with a 12-mer DNA duplex containing 5-methylcytosine (5CM).

06 / Critical Evaluation

Analytical Discussion & Key Insights

A rigorous comparison addressing algorithmic congruence, biological fidelity, rogue taxon artifacts, and nucleotide vs protein resolution.

6.1

Methodological Consistency

Do the three algorithms agree?

All three algorithms recover the same high-level topology: the five vertebrate classes are monophyletic, the four mammalian clades are correctly resolved, and the vertebrate grade (fish → amphibia → reptiles/birds → mammals) is preserved. This high congruence reflects strong purifying selection acting on the BEN domain.

NJ vs. UPGMA Distortions

UPGMA equalizes rates, artificially compressing the long Mus musculus branch (rodents evolve ~2.5x faster than mammalian average).

NJ vs. MP & "Rogue Taxa"

MP places Bos taurus slightly closer to Euarchontoglires due to homoplasy in BEN domain character states. Model-based NJ corrects for this.

6.2

Taxonomic Accuracy

Does the tree match known biology?

The inferred trees closely match established vertebrate phylogenomics (Hedges & Kumar, 2009). The ~1% divergence between human and chimpanzee reflects their ~6 Mya split. Anas georgica and Gallus gallus properly group within Diapsida (Aves), while Danio rerio represents the most ancient outgroup (~450 Mya divergence).

08 / Summary

Conclusions & Future Directions

This analysis establishes BEND7 as an exceptionally robust phylogenetic marker across vertebrate lineages while providing a validated amplification cloning strategy for Anas georgica BEND7 expression in pET-28a. Purifying selection on the BEN domain preserves evolutionary signals, and directional BamHI/XhoI cloning allows seamless vector construction.
